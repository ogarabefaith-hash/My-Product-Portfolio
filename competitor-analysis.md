# Competitor Feature Analysis: Event Tech & Vendor Platforms

## Executive Summary
This document provides a strategic breakdown comparing **Evendy** against major global platforms (**Eventbrite** and **Luma**) to evaluate key features, product gaps, and growth opportunities across ticketing, vendor management, and event ecosystem tools.

## Strategic Product Insights

1. **Unfair Advantage (Vendor Marketplace & Financing):** 
   * Evendy sets itself apart from traditional ticketing platforms like Eventbrite by integrating **end-to-end event execution**, including pre-vetted vendor sourcing and event financing.

2. **Growth Opportunity (Community Features):**
   * Platforms like Luma succeed through social-first event pages and attendee network features. Evendy can boost viral adoption by expanding post-event networking and attendee engagement tools.
